# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public deployment landing page served at `/`
- Crawl/discovery files for the deployment domain (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`)
- Public HTML metadata, social metadata, canonicalization, and favicon behavior for the deployment landing page

## Out of scope
- Native mobile app screens under `artifacts/dar-altamaiz-tours/app/**` when evaluating web crawlability
- Expo manifests served for native clients (`/manifest` with `expo-platform` headers)
- External website content loaded from `https://dt-tours.com/` inside the app WebView, because that site is not implemented in this repository
- Internal mockup sandbox files under `artifacts/mockup-sandbox/**`

## Target audience
- Prospective travel customers looking for Dar AlTamaiz Tours on the web and users trying to open the mobile app preview

## Primary keywords
- Unknown — likely brand-led queries around Dar AlTamaiz Tours / D.T. Tours plus travel and booking terms
